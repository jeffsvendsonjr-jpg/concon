# ConCon — Positioning (Three Doors)

Same product, three landing pages, three tones. Same underlying feature
set, different first paragraph, different hero screenshot, different
call-to-action.

---

## Door 1 — Enterprise & Regulated

**Headline:** Auditable conversation state. Nothing leaves the browser.

**Subhead:** ConCon is the compliance layer for teams that use ChatGPT
for work. It maintains a local ledger of every commitment, claim, and
disagreement in a conversation — so you always have a record of what
was actually agreed, and your legal team never has to argue about it.

**Three proof points:**
1. **Strictly local.** No backend. No telemetry. No API keys. Data
   stays in the analyst's browser. Passes SOC 2 CC6 without a review.
2. **Deterministic audit trail.** The ConCon Check report is a
   Markdown/PDF document your compliance team can attach to a matter,
   a case file, or an incident review.
3. **On-device only.** Your General Counsel doesn't need to approve a
   new vendor. There is no vendor.

**Primary CTA:** *Request a walkthrough for your team*

**Who this door is for:**
Legal ops, in-house counsel, financial services compliance, healthcare
providers exploring clinical AI, government contractors, journalists at
regulated newsrooms.

**Pricing signal:** *"Contact us for team licensing."* Do not show a
price on this door. Enterprise buyers who see $12/mo assume the product
isn't serious.

## Door 2 — Individual Professionals

**Headline:** Never lose the thread of a long AI conversation.

**Subhead:** After 40 turns with ChatGPT, do you actually remember
what you agreed to build? What the assistant claimed you decided? What
you dismissed and what you're still on the hook for? ConCon quietly
watches the conversation and holds the answers.

**Three proof points:**
1. **Every commitment gets a card.** Yours, the assistant's, and every
   pushback. You confirm or drop each one — the record follows.
2. **One-click audit.** *ConCon Check* scans the whole conversation
   and hands you a clean report of what's resolved, what's sitting
   open, and what got pushed back on.
3. **It stays yours.** Your ledger never leaves your browser. No
   account, no login, no cloud.

**Primary CTA:** *Try free for this ChatGPT conversation*

**Who this door is for:**
Consultants, senior engineers, product managers running discovery,
lawyers drafting with AI, journalists interviewing sources through
ChatGPT, researchers running long analysis threads.

**Pricing signal:** *"Free forever for personal use. Pro $12/mo for
custom rule packs and cross-conversation memory."*

## Door 3 — Neurodivergent & Accessibility

**Headline:** Externalize what you agreed to, so your brain doesn't
have to hold it.

**Subhead:** Long conversations with AI are exhausting when your
working memory is already spending 80% of its budget on the
conversation itself. ConCon holds the record for you. Every commitment,
every claim, every "wait, did we decide that already?" — visible on
the side of the screen, always there when you scroll back for it.

**Three proof points:**
1. **The ledger is your outboard memory.** Every commitment is a card.
   Skim, confirm, dismiss. The pattern of the conversation becomes
   visible instead of felt.
2. **You decide what counts, at your pace.** Nothing is auto-confirmed
   without your say-so — unless you turn on Trust mode, and even then
   only firm, unhedged statements. No surprises, no overwhelm.
3. **Nothing gets shared.** Your ledger, your rules, your business.
   No login, no account, no cloud sync you didn't opt into.

**Primary CTA:** *Install free — takes 30 seconds*

**Who this door is for:**
Adults with ADHD, autistic users, users with working memory issues,
users recovering from concussion / long COVID cognitive fatigue,
anyone who's said "I know I read this but I can't hold it in my head."

**Pricing signal:** *"Free. Always. Optional Pro tier if you want to
support development."* This audience has been sold to too many times
in exploitative ways. Trust > conversion optimization.

## Cross-cutting rules for all three doors

1. **Never say "AI-powered."** ConCon does deterministic heuristic
   extraction and is transitioning to a bundled local model. The word
   "AI" evokes a server call — the opposite of what we're selling.
2. **Lead with what stays local, not with features.** Every door's
   subhead includes some variant of "in the browser" or "nothing leaves."
3. **Show the panel screenshot.** Nothing sells this product like
   seeing the sidebar with a real long conversation next to it.
4. **CHECK report is your killer demo.** A screenshot of the PASS /
   REVIEW report converts better than any feature list.
5. **No dark patterns.** No "3 days left in trial", no fake urgency,
   no email capture wall. Doctrine.

## Ordering suggestion

- **Phase 1 (v0.5.x — now):** ship Door 2 (individual pros). It's your
  fastest-converting audience and the one most likely to make noise
  online. Product-market fit signal.
- **Phase 2 (v0.6+):** add Door 3 (neurodivergent) once you have 2–3
  testimonials. Build community-first, not marketing-first, for this
  audience.
- **Phase 3 (v1.0+):** open Door 1 (enterprise) once you have SOC 2
  Type I or a public security whitepaper. Enterprise buyers Google
  everything before they email.
